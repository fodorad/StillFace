.. py:class:: ClipHeads

   Sampled frames of a clip with the assigned head of each person.

   .. attribute:: frame_indices

      Sampled frames of the clip.

   .. attribute:: clip_fps

      Frame rate of the clip.

   .. attribute:: assignment

      Role assignment of the head tracks.

   .. attribute:: heads

      Person mapped to one box per sampled frame, None where not found.


   .. py:method:: reason_missing(camera: str, person: str) -> str

      Why a person has no head on some frame of this clip.

      :param camera: Camera name.
      :param person: ``mother`` or ``baby``.

      :returns: ``roles_ambiguous`` if the roles could not be assigned, else ``occluded`` or
                ``no_head`` (see :func:`~stillface.features.gaze.roles.missing_reason`).

   Track the heads of a clip and assign them to the mother and the baby.

   :param clip: Phase clip of one camera.
   :param camera: Camera name.
   :param phase: Experiment phase.
   :param tracker: Head detector and tracker.
   :param config: Gaze configuration (sample rate, role rules).
   :param references: Identity embeddings of the participants, if available.
   :param overrides: Pinned role assignments.

   :returns: Sampled frames, role assignment and per-frame heads.
