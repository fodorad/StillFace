stillface.features.gaze.exordium_heads
======================================

.. py:module:: stillface.features.gaze.exordium_heads

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Head detection and tracking with exordium.

   Faces are found with exordium's YOLO11 face detector on the sampled frames only and
   followed with its IoU tracker. Needs the ``features`` extra (``exordium``).

   The small model at 1280 px finds the mother and the baby on all cameras of the development
   sessions with scores around 0.7-0.9; the nano model at the default 640 px lost the baby on
   the window camera (scores below 0.15). Because a baby's face in profile still scores low on
   some frames, tracks are started by confident detections but continue through weak ones.

   :class:`~stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTracker` backed by exordium.

   :param device_id: Torch device index; negative selects the CPU, otherwise MPS or CUDA if available.
   :param model_name: exordium YOLO11 face model.
   :param imgsz: Detector input size in pixels (long side).
   :param conf: Detector confidence threshold; weak detections are kept for track continuity.
   :param start_score: A track needs confident detections (at least this score) to be kept.
   :param min_confident: Number of confident detections a track needs.
   :param batch_size: Frames per detector call.
   :param max_lost: Frames a track may be missing before it is closed; at sparse sampling at
                    least three sampling steps are allowed.
   :param iou_threshold: Minimum box overlap to continue a track.
