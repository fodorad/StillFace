.. py:data:: TAGGED_ID_OFFSET
   :type:  int
   :value: 1000


   Track ids of role-tagged tracks start here so they never collide with anonymous ones.

.. py:function:: overlaps(track: stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack, other: stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack, min_iou: float, min_share: float) -> bool

   Whether two tracks mostly follow the same head.

   :param track: First track.
   :param other: Second track.
   :param min_iou: Boxes of the same frame with at least this IoU count as the same head.
   :param min_share: Share of the shared frames that must agree.

   :returns: True if the tracks share frames and agree on at least ``min_share`` of them.


.. py:class:: CombinedHeadTracker(tagged: stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTracker, faces: stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTracker, min_iou: float = 0.3, min_share: float = 0.5)

   Tracks of a role-tagged tracker plus the face tracks that are not the same head.

   :param tagged: Tracker returning role-tagged tracks (text-prompted detector).
   :param faces: Tracker returning anonymous face tracks.
   :param min_iou: Overlap above which a face track duplicates a tagged track.
   :param min_share: Share of shared frames that must overlap to drop a face track.


   .. py:method:: track(clip: pathlib.Path, frame_indices: collections.abc.Sequence[int]) -> list[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack]

      Track heads with both trackers and merge the results.

      :param clip: Video clip.
      :param frame_indices: Frames to analyze, ascending.

      :returns: Tagged tracks (ids offset by :data:`TAGGED_ID_OFFSET`) and the face tracks that
                do not duplicate one of them.



.. py:class:: PerCameraTracker(side: stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTracker, close: stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTracker)

   Routes a clip to the tracker of its camera, read from the ``<camera>_<phase>`` name.

   :param side: Tracker for the side cameras, which see both participants.
   :param close: Tracker for the close cameras, which see one participant.


   .. py:method:: track(clip: pathlib.Path, frame_indices: collections.abc.Sequence[int]) -> list[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack]

      Track heads with the tracker of the clip's camera.

      :param clip: Clip named ``<camera>_<phase>.mp4``.
      :param frame_indices: Frames to analyze, ascending.

      :returns: Head tracks.



.. py:function:: anchor_frames(frame_indices: collections.abc.Sequence[int], clip_fps: float, rate_hz: float) -> list[int]

   Choose the requested frames closest to a regular, slower time grid.

   :param frame_indices: Ascending frames that will be asked for.
   :param clip_fps: Frame rate of the clip.
   :param rate_hz: Rate of the anchor grid, at most the clip rate.

   :returns: Unique anchor frames out of ``frame_indices``; empty if there are none.
