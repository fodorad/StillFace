.. py:function:: link_candidates(candidates: list[list[Candidate]], frames: list[int], max_distance: float = 1.5, max_missed: int = 2) -> list[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack]

   Link the candidates of consecutive frames into tracks.

   Each frame's candidates are matched to the open tracks by the Hungarian algorithm on the
   distance between box centers, measured in head sizes (a head moves less than about one
   and a half of its own size between neighboring samples). Unmatched candidates start new
   tracks; a track is closed after ``max_missed`` consecutive frames without a match.

   :param candidates: Candidates of each frame.
   :param frames: Frame index of each entry of ``candidates``.
   :param max_distance: Largest accepted center distance, in head sizes.
   :param max_missed: Frames a track may stay unmatched before it is closed.

   :returns: One track per head, with boxes and scores on the frames it was matched.


.. py:function:: stitch_tracks(tracks: list[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack], max_gap: int, max_distance: float = 1.5) -> list[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack]

   Join track pieces of the same head that a detection dropout split.

   A track that starts after another one ended, within ``max_gap`` frames and close to
   where the other ended (in head sizes), continues it. Pieces are joined greedily, the
   closest pair first.

   :param tracks: Tracks from :func:`link_candidates`.
   :param max_gap: Largest gap in frames between the end of one piece and the start of the next.
   :param max_distance: Largest center distance between the two ends, in head sizes.

   :returns: The stitched tracks; a stitched track keeps the id of its first piece.


.. py:function:: densify(track: stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack, frame_indices: list[int], max_gap: int) -> stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack

   Fill a track between its detected frames by interpolation.

   :param track: Track detected on a sparse set of frames.
   :param frame_indices: Frames to produce boxes for.
   :param max_gap: Longest gap in frames that is bridged.

   :returns: The track on the requested frames; interpolated frames carry the lower score of
             the two neighboring detections.


.. py:function:: anchor_frames(frame_indices: collections.abc.Sequence[int], clip_fps: float, rate_hz: float) -> list[int]

   Choose the requested frames closest to a regular, slower time grid.
