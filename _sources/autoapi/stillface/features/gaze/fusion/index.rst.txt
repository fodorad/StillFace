.. py:data:: SECONDARY_ID_OFFSET
   :type:  int

   Ids of secondary tracks without a primary partner start here.

.. py:function:: merge_duplicates(tracks: collections.abc.Sequence[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack], min_containment: float = 0.6, min_share: float = 0.5) -> list[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack]

   Merge tracks that follow the same head (e.g. a head box and a box of its face part).

   Two tracks are duplicates if on at least ``min_share`` of their shared frames the
   smaller box lies mostly (``min_containment``) inside the other. A merged track keeps,
   per frame, the box with the higher score.

   :param tracks: Head tracks of one detector.
   :param min_containment: Share of the smaller box inside the other.
   :param min_share: Share of shared frames that must agree.

   :returns: Tracks without duplicates; a merged track keeps the id of the longer one.


.. py:function:: fuse_tracks(primary: collections.abc.Sequence[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack], secondary: collections.abc.Sequence[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack], min_iou: float = 0.1, min_share: float = 0.5) -> list[stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack]

   Merge the tracks of a head detector (primary) and a face detector (secondary).

   A secondary track that follows the same person as a primary track (overlapping boxes on
   most shared frames) is merged into it: on frames where both exist the box is their union
   (a face box lies inside the head box, so the head box mostly wins), the score is the
   larger one and the frame is marked as face visible; frames only one detector saw are
   taken from that detector. Secondary tracks without a partner are kept as additional
   heads.

   :param primary: Head tracks.
   :param secondary: Face tracks.
   :param min_iou: Boxes overlapping at least this much belong to the same person.
   :param min_share: Share of shared frames that must overlap.

   :returns: Fused tracks; ids of unmatched secondary tracks are offset by
             :data:`SECONDARY_ID_OFFSET`.
