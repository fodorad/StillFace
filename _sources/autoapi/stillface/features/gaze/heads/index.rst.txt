.. py:class:: HeadBox

   Axis-aligned head box in normalized image coordinates.

   .. attribute:: x0

      Left edge.

   .. attribute:: y0

      Top edge.

   .. attribute:: x1

      Right edge.

   .. attribute:: y1

      Bottom edge.


   .. py:property:: area
      :type: float


      Box area as a share of the image.


   .. py:property:: center
      :type: tuple[float, float]


      Box center ``(x, y)``.


   .. py:method:: expand(margin: float) -> HeadBox

      Grow the box on every side by a share of its size, clipped to the image.

      :param margin: Relative margin, 0.25 adds a quarter of the width and height per side.

      :returns: The enlarged box.



.. py:class:: HeadTrack

   One head followed across the sampled frames of a clip.

   .. attribute:: track_id

      Identifier of the track within the clip.

   .. attribute:: boxes

      Frame index mapped to the head box, for the frames the head was found in.

   .. attribute:: embedding

      Mean face-identity embedding of the track, None if not computed.

   .. attribute:: scores

      Frame index mapped to the detection confidence; frames without a score count
      as fully confident.

   .. attribute:: role

      ``mother`` or ``baby`` if the detector knows whose head it is (text-prompted
      detectors do), None for anonymous tracks.


   .. py:method:: evidence() -> float

      Confidence-weighted presence: the sum of the detection scores over the frames.



   .. py:method:: weight() -> float

      Confidence-weighted head size: the sum of box area times score over the frames.

.. py:function:: interpolate_boxes(anchors: dict[int, HeadBox], frame_indices: collections.abc.Sequence[int], max_gap: int) -> dict[int, HeadBox]

   Fill the frames between detected anchor frames by linear interpolation.

   Frames before the first or after the last anchor, and frames whose surrounding anchors
   are further apart than ``max_gap`` frames, stay empty.

   :param anchors: Frame index mapped to the detected box.
   :param frame_indices: Frames to produce boxes for.
   :param max_gap: Largest distance in frames between two anchors that is still bridged.

   :returns: Frame index mapped to a box, for every requested frame that could be filled.
