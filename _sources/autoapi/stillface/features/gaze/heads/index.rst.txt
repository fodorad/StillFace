.. py:class:: HeadBox

   Axis-aligned head box in normalized image coordinates.

   .. attribute:: x0

      Left edge.

   .. attribute:: y0

      Top edge.

   .. attribute:: x1

      Right edge.

   .. attribute:: y1

      Bottom edge.


   .. py:property:: area
      :type: float


      Box area as a share of the image.


   .. py:property:: center
      :type: tuple[float, float]


      Box center ``(x, y)``.


   .. py:method:: expand(margin: float) -> HeadBox

      Grow the box on every side by a share of its size, clipped to the image.

      :param margin: Relative margin, 0.25 adds a quarter of the width and height per side.

      :returns: The enlarged box.



.. py:class:: HeadTrack

   One head followed across the sampled frames of a clip.

   .. attribute:: track_id

      Identifier of the track within the clip.

   .. attribute:: boxes

      Frame index mapped to the head box, for the frames the head was found in.

   .. attribute:: embedding

      Mean face-identity embedding of the track, None if not computed.

   .. attribute:: scores

      Frame index mapped to the detection confidence; frames without a score count
      as fully confident.

   .. attribute:: faces

      Frame index mapped to the face detector's confidence, for the frames on which a
      face was detected in this head (the face is visible).


   .. py:method:: face_share() -> float

      Share of the track's frames on which the face was detected.



   .. py:method:: evidence(centrality: bool = False) -> float

      Confidence-weighted presence: the sum of the detection scores over the frames.

      :param centrality: Also weight each frame by how close the box center is to the image
                         center (1 at the center, 0 at the edge midpoints and beyond).

      :returns: The weighted sum.



   .. py:method:: median_score() -> float

      Median detection score of the track (frames without a score count as 1).



   .. py:method:: median_area() -> float

      Median box area of the track, 0 for an empty track.



   .. py:method:: weight() -> float

      Confidence-weighted head size: the sum of box area times score over the frames.

.. py:function:: interpolate_boxes(anchors: dict[int, HeadBox], frame_indices: collections.abc.Sequence[int], max_gap: int) -> dict[int, HeadBox]

   Fill the frames between detected anchor frames by linear interpolation.

   Frames before the first or after the last anchor, and frames whose surrounding anchors
   are further apart than ``max_gap`` frames, stay empty.

   :param anchors: Frame index mapped to the detected box.
   :param frame_indices: Frames to produce boxes for.
   :param max_gap: Largest distance in frames between two anchors that is still bridged.

   :returns: Frame index mapped to a box, for every requested frame that could be filled.


.. py:function:: iou(a: HeadBox, b: HeadBox) -> float

   Intersection over union of two boxes.


.. py:function:: containment(a: HeadBox, b: HeadBox) -> float

   Share of the smaller box covered by the other box.


.. py:function:: overlaps(track: HeadTrack, other: HeadTrack, min_iou: float, min_share: float) -> bool

   Whether two tracks mostly follow the same head.

   :param track: First track.
   :param other: Second track.
   :param min_iou: Boxes of the same frame with at least this IoU count as the same head.
   :param min_share: Share of the shared frames that must agree.

   :returns: True if the tracks share frames and agree on at least ``min_share`` of them.
