stillface.features.audio.workers.silero
=======================================

.. py:module:: stillface.features.audio.workers.silero

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Silero VAD worker: speech probability per 32 ms chunk (environment ``vad``).

   Silero is trained on speech; how it treats infant cries is untested, which is why it is
   only a second opinion next to the VTC voice-type union.

.. py:function:: postprocess(probs: numpy.ndarray, rate: int, chunk: int = CHUNK) -> stillface.features.audio.models.FrameScores

   Turn chunk probabilities into frame scores at the chunk centres.

   :param probs: Speech probability per chunk, shape ``(n,)``.
   :param rate: Sample rate.
   :param chunk: Samples per chunk.

   :returns: One ``speech`` score per chunk, centred at ``(i + 0.5) * chunk / rate``.
