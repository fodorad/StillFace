stillface.features.audio.workers.pyannote_worker
================================================

.. py:module:: stillface.features.audio.workers.pyannote_worker

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   pyannote worker: speech activity and speaker embeddings (environment ``vad``).

   Two outputs from the pyannote community-1 models (Bredin et al.):

   - ``speech`` frame scores (whole-phase requests): the segmentation model's per-frame
     speaker activities, the maximum over its local speakers being the probability that anyone
     speaks. It is the third voice-activity opinion next to VTC and Silero.
   - ``embedding`` segment vectors (segment requests): one L2-normalised speaker embedding per
     segment, used to tell the mother from other women by similarity to her enrolled voice.

   The community-1 models are gated on Hugging Face; the token comes from ``HF_TOKEN``.

.. py:function:: max_over_speakers(scores: numpy.ndarray) -> numpy.ndarray

   Speech activity of each chunk frame: the maximum over the local speakers.

   The segmentation model is permutation-invariant (its speaker channels mean nothing
   across chunks), so pyannote only aggregates overlapping chunks after this hook has
   reduced them to one speaker-free channel, as its own voice activity pipeline does.

   :param scores: Chunk outputs, shape ``(chunks, frames, speakers)``.

   :returns: Shape ``(chunks, frames, 1)``.


.. py:function:: speech_scores(start: float, step: float, duration: float, activity: numpy.ndarray) -> stillface.features.audio.models.FrameScores

   Speech probability from aggregated per-frame activities.

   :param start: Start of the first frame.
   :param step: Frame step.
   :param duration: Frame duration.
   :param activity: Aggregated activities, shape ``(n,)`` or ``(n, channels)``.

   :returns: ``speech`` = maximum activity, at the frame centres, clipped to ``[0, 1]``.

   :raises ValueError: If the activities are still per chunk (3-D, not aggregated).


.. py:function:: embedding_scores(segments: tuple[stillface.features.audio.models.Segment, ...], vectors: list[numpy.ndarray | None]) -> stillface.features.audio.models.SegmentScores

   L2-normalised embeddings per segment.

   :param segments: Requested segments.
   :param vectors: One vector per segment, None where it could not be computed.

   :returns: Segment scores with ``duration_s`` as value and the embeddings (NaN rows for None).

   :raises ValueError: If the counts disagree.
