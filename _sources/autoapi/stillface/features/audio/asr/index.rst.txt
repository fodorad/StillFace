stillface.features.audio.asr
============================

.. py:module:: stillface.features.audio.asr

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   ASR stage: what the mother said, when, and which words to distrust.

   CrisperWhisper 2 runs on the mother-only (time-gated) track. Whisper models may "hear"
   words in silence or in the baby's vocalisations, so every word whose midpoint falls outside
   the padded mother segments is flagged ``outside_mother``. The verbatim words of each mother
   segment form one utterance for the Montreal Forced Aligner, which gives word and phone
   timing (speech rate, vowel measures).

   This module also chooses the request builder of every model from its config
   (:func:`builder_for`), so the CLI and the full chain share one wiring.

.. py:function:: utterances(words: collections.abc.Sequence[stillface.features.audio.models.Segment], intervals: collections.abc.Sequence[tuple[float, float]]) -> list[stillface.features.audio.models.Segment]

   Group words into one utterance per kept interval, for the aligner.

   Event tags (``[laughter]``) stay in the transcript but are left out here: they have no
   pronunciation, and an utterance with nothing else to align breaks MFA's export.

   :param words: Word segments (label = word), phase time.
   :param intervals: Padded mother intervals.

   :returns: One segment per interval with words, its label the words joined by spaces.

.. py:function:: builder_for(model: stillface.features.audio.config.ModelConfig, config: stillface.features.audio.config.AudioConfig) -> stillface.features.audio.infer.RequestBuilder

   Request builder of a configured model.
