AudioSet classes (AST)¶

AST scores all 527 AudioSet classes in every 2 s window (0.25 s hop); nothing is filtered before or during inference, and the raw scores of every class are kept in the raw output. The priority tiers below only say which classes the analysis looks at first:

  • Tier 1, target vocalisations: what the mother and the baby produce (cry, fuss, laugh, babble, singing, humming, speech by sex and age).

  • Tier 2, context: the phase-change gong, bodily sounds, touch and play sounds, and background sounds that can mask the dyad.

  • Tier 0: everything else (animals, vehicles, music genres …).

Every tier 1-2 class gets its own probability lane on the 10 Hz fusion grid (ast.<label>.context, set by fusion.ast_lane_max_tier). The table ships with the package as stillface/features/audio/ast_classes.csv (index, label, tier, group, reason) and can be filtered by tier or group in any spreadsheet.
