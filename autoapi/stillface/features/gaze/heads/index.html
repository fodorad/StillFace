class stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadTrack[source]¶

One head followed across the sampled frames of a clip.

track_id¶

Identifier of the track within the clip.

boxes¶

Frame index mapped to the head box, for the frames the head was found in.

embedding¶

Mean face-identity embedding of the track, None if not computed.

scores¶

Frame index mapped to the detection confidence; frames without a score count as fully confident.

faces¶

Frame index mapped to the face detector’s confidence, for the frames on which a face was detected in this head (the face is visible).

face_share() → float[source]¶

Share of the track’s frames on which the face was detected.

evidence(centrality: bool = False) → float[source]¶

Confidence-weighted presence: the sum of the detection scores over the frames.

Parameters:

centrality – Also weight each frame by how close the box center is to the image center (1 at the center, 0 at the edge midpoints and beyond).

Returns:

The weighted sum.

median_score() → float[source]¶

Median detection score of the track (frames without a score count as 1).

median_area() → float[source]¶

Median box area of the track, 0 for an empty track.

weight() → float[source]¶

Confidence-weighted head size: the sum of box area times score over the frames.

stillface.features.gaze.heads.interpolate_boxes(anchors: dict[int, HeadBox], frame_indices: collections.abc.Sequence[int], max_gap: int) → dict[int, HeadBox][source]¶

Fill the frames between detected anchor frames by linear interpolation.

Frames before the first or after the last anchor, and frames whose surrounding anchors are further apart than max_gap frames, stay empty.

Parameters:

  • anchors – Frame index mapped to the detected box.

  • frame_indices – Frames to produce boxes for.

  • max_gap – Largest distance in frames between two anchors that is still bridged.

Returns:

Frame index mapped to a box, for every requested frame that could be filled.

stillface.features.gaze.heads.overlaps(track: HeadTrack, other: HeadTrack, min_iou: float, min_share: float) → bool[source]¶

Whether two tracks mostly follow the same head.

Parameters:

  • track – First track.

  • other – Second track.

  • min_iou – Boxes of the same frame with at least this IoU count as the same head.

  • min_share – Share of the shared frames that must agree.

Returns:

True if the tracks share frames and agree on at least min_share of them.
