stillface.features.gaze.config¶

Configuration of the gaze extraction.

Every threshold, margin and prior lives here, never in the code, and a copy is stored in each run record. Values are calibrated on the development sessions and versioned through the version key of the config file.

Attributes¶

CONFIG_VERSION

Supported version of the gaze config file format.

DEFAULT_CONFIG_PATH

Config file shipped with the package.

class stillface.features.gaze.config.SidePriors[source]¶

Which side of the side cameras’ image the mother sits on, from the fixed seating.

During the baseline the mother sits on a different chair, which flips her side in the window camera; the door camera sees her on the left in every phase.

window¶

left or right, play, still face and reunion.

window_baseline¶

left or right, baseline.

door¶

left or right, play, still face and reunion.

door_baseline¶

left or right, baseline.

side(camera: str, phase: str) → str[source]¶

Side of the mother in a camera during a phase.

class stillface.features.gaze.config.Thresholds[source]¶

Decision thresholds of the class assignment.

partner_mass¶

Heatmap share on the partner needed for the PARTNER class.

inout¶

In/out-of-frame score below which the gaze target is out of frame.

box_margin¶

Relative margin added around the partner’s head box.

class stillface.features.gaze.config.RoleRules[source]¶

Rules assigning head tracks to the mother and the baby.

min_presence¶

Share of the sampled frames a track must appear in to be a candidate.

min_side_separation¶

Smaller horizontal distance between the two candidates than this (normalized) makes the side prior ambiguous.

min_relative_head_area¶

On the side cameras a head must be at least this share of the largest head’s area; smaller heads are far-away background people.

min_owner_score¶

On the close cameras the owner’s track needs at least this median detection score (rejects head-like drawings when the person is away).

min_owner_area¶

On the close cameras the owner’s track needs at least this median box area (the owner sits close to the camera).
