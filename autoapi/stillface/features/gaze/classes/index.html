stillface.features.gaze.classes¶

From gaze heatmaps to the plan’s gaze target classes.

Classes follow the manual coding scheme: for the baby (T1) ANYARA (at the mother), TARGY_KORNYEZET (at an object or the environment), ELFORDUL (turned away); for the mother (T6) BABARA (at the baby) and MASHOVA (elsewhere); NK if not codable.

stillface.features.gaze.classes.classify(person: str, camera: str, heatmap: numpy.ndarray, inout: float | None, partner_box: stillface.features.gaze.heads.HeadBox | None, config: stillface.features.gaze.config.GazeConfig) → GazeDecision[source]¶

Turn one person’s gaze heatmap into a target class.

Side cameras see the partner, so looking at the partner means heatmap mass inside the partner’s (enlarged) head box. A close camera does not see the partner; there the calibrated partner zone of that camera takes its place.

Parameters:

  • person – mother or baby, the looker.

  • camera – Camera the frame comes from.

  • heatmap – Gaze map of shape (height, width).

  • inout – Probability that the target is inside the frame, None if unavailable.

  • partner_box – Head box of the partner on side cameras, None if not found.

  • config – Thresholds, margins and partner zones.

Returns:

The decision with the supporting values.

Raises:

ValueError – If person or camera is unknown.
