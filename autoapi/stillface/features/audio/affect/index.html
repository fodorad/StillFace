stillface.features.audio.affect¶

Affect lanes: arousal and valence of the mother and the baby.

Mother (adult speech models, acceptable for her):

  • audEERING MSP-dim arousal ([0, 1]) and valence (rescaled to [-1, 1]);

  • the emotion2vec+ cross-check, derived from its categories: valence = p(happy) - (p(sad) + p(angry) + p(fearful) + p(disgusted)) and arousal = 1 - p(neutral);

  • per session, the Spearman agreement of the two models on each dimension. Low agreement means the session’s maternal affect estimates should not be trusted.

Baby (no released infant valence / arousal model exists):

  • derived valence = sum(weight * p(type)) with the configured weights (default -1 p(cry) - 0.5 p(fuss) + 1 p(laugh)), clipped to [-1, 1];

  • derived arousal = mean of the within-session min-max scaled loudness, F0 and p(cry);

  • audEERING on baby windows, written as a separate lane marked experimental.

Every lane is NaN outside the segments of its role. Window scores are spread over the grid bins their window covers (the mean of overlapping windows).
