stillface.features.audio.export¶

Exports: Annie files, QC records and the dataset tables.

Per session and phase:

  • annie/<sid>_<phase>.json: events in Annie’s own nested export shape (video_id -> {fps, tracks: {track: [event, ...]}, meta}); a track is <role>:<source> (e.g. baby:fusion) and bands become events too (voice activity, silence, gong, overlap, roles);

  • annie/<sid>_<phase>_lanes.csv + _lanes.json: every lane and band (fusion and affect) on the 10 Hz grid, with its legend.

Per session qc.json (stage completeness, coverage, disagreement, flags), and for the dataset qc_summary.csv plus listening.csv (every flagged item exactly once).
