stillface.features.audio.asr¶

ASR stage: what the mother said, when, and which words to distrust.

CrisperWhisper 2 runs on the mother-only (time-gated) track. Whisper models may “hear” words in silence or in the baby’s vocalisations, so every word whose midpoint falls outside the padded mother segments is flagged outside_mother. The verbatim words of each mother segment form one utterance for the Montreal Forced Aligner, which gives word and phone timing (speech rate, vowel measures).

This module also chooses the request builder of every model from its config (builder_for()), so the CLI and the full chain share one wiring.
