Audio model outputs¶

What every model of the audio pipeline returns, as checked by stillface.features.audio.contract (the smoke test and the opt-in integration tests run each model in its real environment and test its result against this table). Raw results are kept per session and phase in features/audio/raw/<model>/<phase>.npz, at the model’s own rate; the fusion stage puts them on the common 10 Hz grid.
